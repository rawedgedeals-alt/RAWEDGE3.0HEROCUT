Casino Bonus Wagering Requirements: How to Read the Numbers

A practical explanation of wagering multipliers, bonus restrictions, and why a headline percentage is only the starting point for evaluating an offer.

The headline is not the whole offer

A bonus advertised as a percentage of a deposit can be attractive on the surface, but the percentage alone does not tell you what conditions apply. Before deciding whether an offer is relevant, look for the wagering requirement, eligible games, withdrawal restrictions, maximum cashout, expiration date and jurisdiction rules. These details can matter more than the size of a promotional headline.

What a wagering multiplier means

A wagering multiplier describes how much play an operator requires before certain bonus-linked funds may be withdrawn, if withdrawal is allowed under that offer. The calculation may be based on the bonus amount, the deposit plus bonus, or another basis explicitly defined by the operator. Do not assume two offers use the same calculation.

For a purely hypothetical example, imagine a $50 bonus with a 30-times bonus-only wagering requirement. The stated playthrough would be $50 multiplied by 30, or $1,500 in total qualifying wagers. That is not a prediction of how much money you will win or lose, and it does not mean a player should attempt to wager that amount. It is only an illustration of how the requirement is expressed.

Check what counts toward the requirement

Some bonus terms may treat slots, table games and other games differently. A game might count only part of each wager, or not count at all. Operators may also set bet-size caps while a bonus is active. Breaking those rules may affect eligibility for withdrawal. The operator's written terms, rather than a banner, should be the reference point.

Look for limits beyond wagering

Other conditions can include a maximum amount that can be withdrawn from bonus-related winnings, minimum or maximum deposit amounts, an expiration deadline, identity verification and restrictions by state or country. A no-deposit or free-spins promotion can still include conditions. Eligibility and age requirements can also differ by jurisdiction.

Compare using actual terms

Write down the offer's calculation basis, multiplier, permitted games, bet limit, cashout cap and deadline. If a clause is missing or hard to find, treat the offer as unclear rather than filling the gap with a guess.
