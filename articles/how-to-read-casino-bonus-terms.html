Our bonus terms glossary explains what each term means. This page is the companion skill: how to open an actual offer's terms and find the clauses that decide what the bonus really costs and what you can actually withdraw. Work through the steps in order — each one looks for a specific line in the operator's own document.

RawEdge rule: the operator's current terms are the only version that counts. Promotions change, and screenshots of old offers prove nothing. Save or screenshot the terms on the day you opt in.

Step 1 — Find the wagering requirement and its exact calculation

The wagering (playthrough) requirement describes how much eligible play may be required before bonus-related funds become withdrawable. The number alone is not enough: find the operator's exact calculation. Terms differ on whether the multiplier applies to the bonus only, or to the deposit plus the bonus — the same headline number can mean very different amounts of play. Write the formula down in plain words before you continue.

Step 2 — Check which games count, and how much

Different games can contribute differently — or not at all — to promotional requirements. Look for the contribution table or the eligible-games clause. If the games you actually play contribute little or nothing, the wagering figure from Step 1 understates your real cost. Never assume every game counts equally.

Step 3 — Find the maximum bet while wagering

Many promotions limit the size of a single bet while a bonus is active, and bets above the limit can void the bonus and its winnings. Find that limit now, not after a big win. If the terms you are reading do not state one, note the gap — an unclear term is a reason to ask the operator for its written explanation before depositing.

Step 4 — Find the deadline, and what happens when it passes

Bonuses and promotional balances may have deadlines. Check the exact time period and what happens to an unused or incomplete offer: whether remaining bonus funds and any winnings from them are removed. A generous wagering deal with a short clock can be worse than a stricter one with time to finish.

Step 5 — Find the maximum cashout

Some promotions cap the amount that can be withdrawn from promotional winnings. Find the specific cap before opting in. If there is a cap, it — not the bonus size — is the ceiling on what the offer can ever pay you.

Step 6 — Check the deposit and withdrawal conditions attached to the offer

Look for minimum deposits, eligible payment methods, identity verification requirements and restrictions that apply when you request a withdrawal. Some offers treat deposits made by particular methods differently. These clauses live at the end of the terms document and are the ones players most often meet for the first time at the cashier.

Step 7 — Do the arithmetic on your own numbers

Now combine what you found: the wagering formula from Step 1, the contribution rate of the games you play from Step 2, and the deadline from Step 4. Ask whether the required play is realistic for your normal stakes and schedule. Our bonus wagering calculator and the deep-dive on how wagering requirements work can help you sanity-check the math. If the honest answer is "I would have to play more, faster, or bigger than I planned," the bonus is not a deal — it is a commitment.

The 60-second pre-deposit checklist

  • Wagering formula found and understood (bonus only, or deposit + bonus)?
  • Games I play actually contribute — at what rate?
  • Maximum bet while wagering noted?
  • Expiry date noted — and what happens to winnings if I run out of time?
  • Maximum cashout on promotional winnings found?
  • Deposit method and withdrawal conditions checked?
  • Terms saved or screenshotted today?

Any box you cannot tick is a question for the operator — or a reason to skip the offer. A bonus you have not read is a price you have not seen.
