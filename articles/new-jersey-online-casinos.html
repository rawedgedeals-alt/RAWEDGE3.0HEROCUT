The official source: NJ Division of Gaming Enforcement

The NJ Division of Gaming Enforcement publishes an official list of approved internet gaming sites. Brands on that list include BetMGM, DraftKings, BetRivers and other approved operators. If a real-money online casino taking New Jersey players is not on the Division's list, that absence is the answer to your research question — do not let a marketing page argue otherwise.

How to verify a New Jersey casino in three steps

  1. Find the site on the NJ DGE approved list. Use the regulator's published list, not a logo on the operator's homepage. A logo or statement on a marketing page is not a substitute for official confirmation.
  2. Read the operator's own terms for New Jersey. Check the sections on age, residence and physical location, plus the bonus terms, payment methods and withdrawal conditions that apply to your account. Our bonus terms walkthrough shows how to work through them.
  3. Keep a dated record. Save the regulator link you checked and the date. Approvals and offers change; your record shows what was publicly stated when you checked.
