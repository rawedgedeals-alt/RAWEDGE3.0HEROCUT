Pennsylvania is one of the states with licensed, regulated real-money online casinos — which makes it one of the easiest states to research properly, because the regulator publishes exactly which operators are licensed. This page shows you where that official list lives, how to use it, and how to keep licensed Pennsylvania casinos separate from sweepstakes and offshore offers that are not on it.

The official source: Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board

The Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board publishes an official list of licensed interactive gaming operators. Brands on that list include BetMGM, DraftKings, BetRivers and other licensed operators. If a real-money online casino taking Pennsylvania players is not on the Board's list, that absence is the answer to your research question — do not let a marketing page argue otherwise.

How to verify a Pennsylvania casino in three steps

  1. Find the site on the PGCB licensed operator list. Use the regulator's published list, not a logo on the operator's homepage. A logo or statement on a marketing page is not a substitute for official confirmation.
  2. Read the operator's own terms for Pennsylvania. Check the sections on age, residence and physical location, plus the bonus terms, payment methods and withdrawal conditions that apply to your account. Our bonus terms walkthrough shows how to work through them.
  3. Keep a dated record. Save the regulator link you checked and the date. Approvals and offers change; your record shows what was publicly stated when you checked.

Is online casino gambling legal in Pennsylvania?
Pennsylvania licenses and regulates real-money online casinos through the Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board, which publishes the licensed interactive gaming operator list. Only operators on that list are licensed in Pennsylvania — verify there first, every time.

Can I play at a Pennsylvania casino from another state?
Licensed online casinos apply location rules; eligibility depends on where you physically are and the operator's terms, not just where your account was opened. Check the operator's current terms and the regulator's guidance rather than assuming.
