How to check a bonus before claiming it

  1. Find the exact wagering base. “30× bonus” and “30× deposit plus bonus” can produce very different amounts.
  2. Check eligible games and weighting. Some games may contribute less than their full stake—or not count at all.
  3. Check the deadline. Look for an expiration date and whether the requirement can realistically be completed.
  4. Read withdrawal and cashout rules. Maximum cashout limits, verification, payment exclusions and minimums can matter.
  5. Confirm local eligibility. State laws, licensing, age restrictions and the type of platform may affect whether an offer is available.
