Casino bonus terms, decoded.

The terms that matter most before you claim a promotional offer.

Wagering requirement

This describes how much eligible play may be required before bonus-related funds become withdrawable. Always read the operator's exact calculation and qualifying-game rules.

Expiration

Bonuses and promotional balances may have deadlines. Check the exact time period and what happens to an unused or incomplete offer.

Maximum cashout

Some promotions cap the amount that can be withdrawn from promotional winnings. Find the specific cap before opting in.

Eligible games

Different games can contribute differently—or not at all—to promotional requirements. Never assume every game counts equally.

Deposit and withdrawal conditions

Look for minimum deposits, eligible payment methods, identity verification and restrictions that apply when you request a withdrawal.

Before you click

Take a screenshot or save the current terms when an offer matters to you. Promotions can change.
