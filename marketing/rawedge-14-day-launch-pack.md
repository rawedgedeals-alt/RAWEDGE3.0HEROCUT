## Production system
1. Select a sourced fact or labeled hypothetical calculation.
2. Produce a vertical 9:16 video with a readable hook in the first three seconds.
3. Use one educational point, one RawEdge destination, and a source check.
4. Review legal age, local distribution restrictions, audio rights and platform rules before scheduling.
5. Measure 3-second views, watch retention, profile visits, outbound clicks, and GA4 engaged sessions. Never confuse impressions with verified visitors.
6. Feed measured audience numbers into advertiser proposals without inflating them.

## Brand rhythm
Two short educational reels, one screenshot or text carousel, and one independent research update per week is the minimum repeatable cadence; seven days above are a launch sprint, not a promise of unrestricted platform distribution. Reuse successful formats, but don't recycle unsourced claims.
