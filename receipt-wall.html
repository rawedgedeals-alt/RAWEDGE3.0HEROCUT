What these labels really mean

Research profile published: We have an informational page with operator-published material and questions to investigate. It does not mean we endorse the operator or have tested its payouts.

Primary sources verified: We have independently checked the cited original source documents and recorded evidence. Until then, a research page remains a work in progress.

Withdrawal tested: We completed and documented a real withdrawal. If that has not happened, we display not tested, not a guessed processing time.
